Most corrections are accepted. Three blockers remain.

1. **DISAGREE: GC still has a restored-snapshot race.**  
   A failed integration→App fetch does not prove the App is down. The App may retain a fresh pre-restart report acknowledging a newer catalog. The restored integration can then stat/select an old render while GC deletes it, before its queued pin arrives ([lines 169–182](/Users/jdavidoa91/Dev/hass-cinema-studio/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-cinema-studio-design.md:169)).

   **Required:** coordinate GC with selection through a shared durable pin/fence under a cross-process lock, or require successful startup reconciliation before restored-snapshot selection and explicitly narrow the offline guarantee. The shared mechanism preserves the intended offline behavior.

   Also give reports a consumer generation and monotonic sequence; reject delayed reports from earlier generations/sequences. Arrival time alone cannot establish which snapshot/pin set is latest. Specify the catalog revision in selection reports explicitly.

2. **DISAGREE: orphan recovery can invalidate selected media after an App database restore.**  
   Moving every render lacking a DB row immediately makes its URI disappear—even if HA’s persisted snapshot or an active session references it ([lines 135–138](/Users/jdavidoa91/Dev/hass-cinema-studio/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-cinema-studio-design.md:135)).

   **Required:** leave unrecognized committed renders at their original paths until reconciled with consumer snapshots/pins and safely retired. Distinguish incomplete staging files from committed files missing database records. Moving a committed render must follow the same protection rules as deletion.

3. **DISAGREE: imported timing still lacks a byte-identity check against Worker metadata.**  
   Idle status plus stable size/mtime during linking proves stability during that interval, not that the fetched timing belongs to those bytes ([lines 140–150](/Users/jdavidoa91/Dev/hass-cinema-studio/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-cinema-studio-design.md:140)). Two renders can have equal durations but different boundaries.

   **Required:** export the Worker’s recorded output fingerprint and compare it with the linked file’s computed hash/size before accepting `legacy_worker` timing. Missing or mismatched fingerprints require quarantine/recompile. Make Worker quiescence an enforced import precondition for the entire operation, including scheduled jobs and edits, rather than one initial idle check.

- Document that independent fallback history can repeat clips already selected through Studio.
